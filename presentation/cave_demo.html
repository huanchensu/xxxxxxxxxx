The Ali Baba Cave: You Are the Verifier

Deep inside a ring-shaped cave lies a magic door. The person across from you claims to know its password. You stay at the entrance, flip coins to issue challenges, and watch whether they always exit from the side you demand. The more rounds you run, the closer a liar's survival odds collapse to zero.

Notice: all you ever saw was "they came out the side you demanded." You never heard one syllable of the password — that is zero-knowledge.

The three moves: Commit (they enter via a random path — unseen) → Challenge (you flip a coin and name an exit) → Response (they emerge, or they don't). An honest prover always succeeds (completeness); a cheater survives each round with probability 1/2, leaving 2⁻ⁿ after n rounds (soundness); and the only information you gain is the single bit "they know the password" (zero-knowledge).
